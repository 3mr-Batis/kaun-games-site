PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: May 29, 2026

KAUN Games ("we", "us", or "our") built the RISE game as a free mobile game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our game.

By using RISE, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information. However, third-party services integrated into the game may collect certain data as described below.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the game. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data and interaction with ads

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings under Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

The game offers an optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") processed entirely through Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Google Play's Payments Privacy Notice applies to all transactions.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require account registration or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.

Children's Privacy

RISE is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Data Retention

The game stores only your high score and sound preferences locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data is never transmitted to our servers and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Security

We value the trust you place in us. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective immediately upon posting to this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
